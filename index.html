Hi Goodreads, i'm Seth Stomberger

Product + Systems Designer · Brooklyn NYC

If you’ve ordered groceries from @Whole Foods Market or @Amazon, the team member who picked your order used a tool Seth designed.

He’s a product and systems designer at Amazon working on the experiences behind grocery fulfillment. He helped build the design system behind grocery handheld tools, led design for a converged shopping experience now shared by Whole Foods Market and fulfillment center team members, and is now injecting AI assistance into these same tools.

Seth cares deeply about visual craft and clear systems, and brings the same standard to every screen, whether a customer or a team member is using it. Outside of work, he’s usually watching theater, practicing yoga, or reading a good book. Say hey!
